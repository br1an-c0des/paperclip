COMPONENT

Request cards

One card for anything waiting on someone's response. Questions, Decisions, approvals, tool reviews and review stages all use it, so a request looks and works the same in the Inbox, a task thread and agent chat. Based on the unifying-requests proposal.

Four kinds

QUESTION
Question

Your answer goes back to the agent, which decides what to do with it.

Questions, confirmations, plan approvals
PROPOSAL
Proposal

Approving applies the change for you, exactly as shown.

Decisions, tool calls, secrets, suggested tasks
APPROVAL
Approval

A formal request, with its own discussion and revisions.

Hires, budget stops, board approvals, strategy
SIGN-OFF
Sign-off

Your turn in a review stage planned on the task.

  1. 1Kind. Question, proposal, approval or sign-off, by colour, icon and label.
  2. 2Subject. The task, tasks, agent, budget or company it is about. Hidden inside a task thread, where it would repeat the page.
  3. 3Lifetime. When it expires, or what ends it. Red when it is close.
  4. 4Who is asking, and why now. The asker and the reason it needs an answer now.
  5. 5The ask. One plain sentence, then the context.
  6. 6Response controls. Shaped by the request: yes or no, pick one, pick several, a form, suggested tasks, or a verdict.
  7. 7What happens next. One line answering "what happens if I click this?"
  8. 8Who can answer. Anyone, people only, or a named person or agent.
  9. 9Snooze and dismiss. In the menu, so they never compete with the answer.

After an answer, and other states
