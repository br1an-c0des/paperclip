Inbox, Needs you

Starts with every card collapsed. Click a row to open it; use Collapse all, the kind filter and Group. With the list focused: j/k or the up and down arrows move, → opens, ← closes (and on a group header folds the group), Shift with an arrow opens or closes them all.

4 · decisions to make

Notes and open questions

Kept in the collapsed row

  • The kind stripe and a coloured type pill, now naming the specific type (Tool call, Plan review, Budget stop) rather than only the kind.
  • The title, on one line (two on a phone).
  • For questions, each question on its own line, up to three, then "+ n more".
  • Who's asking (avatar), the task ID, and the age.
  • Only the indicators that change what you do first: an expiry under a day, Acts, Blocks, Task changed, Waiting on someone, a count, Plan v2, the budget overrun, Outside agent, and New.

Treated as clutter until opened

  • The ask line's sentence ("asks you 3 questions · 40m ago"): the avatar and age carry it.
  • The task's title beside its ID, the Formal tag, "Open until answered" and "Ends if you comment instead".
  • Body text, options and every control, the "what happens next" line, and who can answer.
  • The context column (About HAR-41, why it's here) and the "…" menu. Snooze and clear stay on s and y for the selected row.
