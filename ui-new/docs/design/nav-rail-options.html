Nav rail options

Three ways for the left nav to fold down to icons: Company, Search, Inbox, Status, Projects, Agents and You. Each is drawn at rest in a slice of the org dashboard, then with its labels, menus and lists pinned open. Hover the icons in each slice to try the labels.

Same seven places

Company, Search, Inbox, Status, Projects, Agents, You, in the full nav's order. Status only while enableStatusCards is on, as today.

Same marks

The current page's icon is raised with the --sel pill. Inbox carries its count badge in the corner, capped at 99+. Agents shows when something is running.

Same palette

Search opens the ⌘K palette (Ctrl K off a Mac). New task stays on C and in the palette in every direction.

1

Labels on hover

The plainest rail. Every icon is a link, a label appears beside it on hover, and Projects and Agents go to their pages. Nothing new opens from the rail except the company menu.

Labels
A one-line tooltip, 8px right of the rail and centered on the icon. It opens 400ms after the pointer settles and at once on keyboard focus. Once one is showing, moving along the rail swaps labels instantly until 300ms after the pointer leaves. Fades in over 120ms and is never interactive.
Company
The disc links to the dashboard (G D). Its tooltip shows the company name. On hover a small Repeat button appears on the disc's corner and opens the company menu to the right.
Projects and Agents
Links to /projects and /agents. Each stays raised on every page under it, as the project rows do today.
Running agents
A 7px Ion dot breathing on the Agents icon's corner. Unread chat replies add a count on the opposite corner, as on the phone dock.
You
The avatar opens your menu to the right. The menu gains Show labels, and Hide navigation moves to Shift [.
Collapse and expand
[ now switches between the full nav and the rail. Hiding the nav entirely moves to Shift [ and the You menu. There is no button on the rail.
Dragging
Dragging the seam under 200px snaps to the rail on release; the nav dims past 140px to say so. Dragging the rail's seam right past 140px opens the full nav at its last width. Double-click still resets to 248px.

Hover and click, drawn open

Collapse and expand: the icons stay put

The rail is 68px: the nav's 14px padding either side of a 40px column. That puts every icon, disc and avatar on the same vertical line it has in the full nav (the dashed line), within a pixel.

Folding: labels, counts and section rows fade out over 90ms, then the column narrows over 200ms (cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)) while main widens with it. The Projects and Agents icons fade in where their section headings were. Expanding runs it backwards: width first, then the labels.

Reduced motion: it switches in one frame.

Left out, and where it goes
  • New task: C, the palette, and New task on every task list.
  • Apps: the dashboard's Apps tab and the palette.
  • Starred and Recent tasks: only in the full nav; [ brings it back. The palette could list recent tasks before you type.
  • Plugins' sections and toolbar buttons: only in the full nav.
2

Flyout lists

Recommended

The same rail, but Projects and Agents open their lists beside it: starred first, with task counts and live states. You can reach any project or agent in two clicks without leaving the rail. Click Projects or Agents in the slice to try it.

Labels
As in direction 1: 400ms, then instant along the rail. A label never shows while its flyout is open.
Projects and Agents
Click opens a flyout 8px right of the rail, top-aligned with the icon and lifted to stay in the window. Fades in and slides 6px over 140ms. Arrow keys move through it, Enter opens, Escape closes it and returns focus to the icon. Hovering only shows the label, so lists don't flicker open as you pass.
Inside the flyouts
Starred first, then the rest in your order. Projects show task counts; agents show their state, the running ring, and unread chat counts. Each has its page at the foot (All projects, All tasks; All agents), and Projects keeps its + for New project.
Company
Click goes to the dashboard. Hovering for 300ms opens a hover card: the company with its shortcut, then the other companies, All companies and Create organization. It stays open while the pointer is on it.
Collapse and expand
A round tab appears on the seam when you point at it: chevrons left in the full nav, right on the rail. [ still hides the nav entirely, so nothing you know changes; Shift [ switches full and rail. A hidden nav comes back the way you left it.
Dragging
Under 200px the nav snaps to the rail; keep going past 40px and it hides, as dragging does today. Transition as in direction 1.

Hover and click, drawn open

Left out, and where it goes
  • Starred: at the top of each flyout.
  • New task: C and the palette. Apps: the dashboard's Apps tab and the palette.
  • Recent tasks: the palette, if it learns to list them before you type. Otherwise only in the full nav.
  • Plugins' sections, and their items under each project: only in the full nav.
3

Floating capsule

The nav column goes away and the seven icons float in a capsule over the page's left gutter, like the phone's floating dock stood on end. Labels slide out of the capsule. Projects and Agents peek the whole nav as a card, so nothing is ever more than a click away. Click Projects or Agents in the slice.

Labels
The capsule grows a tab to the right, drawn in --card as part of it, revealed left to right over 140ms after 350ms, then instantly along the capsule. It reads as the capsule saying the name, not a separate tooltip.
Company
Click goes to the dashboard. Its tab shows the name in the display face with a Repeat button inside it that opens the company menu. The tab can be clicked because it is part of the capsule.
Projects and Agents
Click peeks the full nav: a 268px card slides out from behind the capsule over 180ms, scrolled to that section with it tinted in --run-bg for a moment. It floats over main without moving it. Escape, a click outside or picking a row closes it; the pin docks it as the full nav again.
Collapse and expand
[ folds the nav into the capsule instead of hiding it; that replaces today's bare state and its show-nav button. Shift [ hides the capsule too. Expand with [, the peek's pin, or Show the full nav in the You menu.
Dragging
Dragging under 200px folds into the capsule. The capsule has no seam, so it can't be dragged open; that is what the pin and [ are for.
Transition
The column closes over 220ms while the capsule fades in, sliding 8px from the left, starting 60ms in. Expanding: the capsule fades out first, then the column opens.

Hover and click, drawn open

Left out, and where it goes
  • Starred, Recent tasks, plugins' sections and Apps: all in the peek.
  • New task: C and the palette, or the optional + above.
  • Plugins' toolbar buttons: in the peek, under the search row as today.

Recommendation

Build direction 2, Flyout lists, with direction 1's shortcut. The flyouts keep starred projects, task counts and live agents one click away, so folding the nav doesn't cost what made it useful. The rail's geometry is shared with the full nav, so folding never moves an icon.

  • [ switches between the full nav and the rail. With a rail, hiding the nav entirely is the rarer need, so it moves to Shift [ and the You menu.
  • Dragging under 200px snaps to the rail, and further, past 40px, hides it.
  • The seam tab as the visible control, so the rail can be found without the shortcut.
  • Company: the hover card. It turns Switch company into one hover and one click, with no tiny target.
  • Keep direction 3 in mind as a separate distraction-free mode, since it matches the phone's floating dock.

Open questions

  1. Settings sidebars. App settings and Company settings put their own sidebar in the nav slot, with the same width, handle and hiding. Should they fold to a rail too (section icons with labels), or stay full width while the main nav is a rail?
  2. The [ key. Should it fold to the rail and leave hiding to Shift [, as recommended, or keep hiding and give the rail its own shortcut?
  3. Narrow windows. Should the nav fold to the rail by itself below a width, say 1100px on a tablet or a split window, and open again when there's room? Or only when you ask?
  4. Flyouts on hover. Click only, as drawn, or should the flyouts also open after a longer hover, like the company card?
  5. Plugins. Plugins can add nav sections, items under each project, and toolbar buttons. Are they fine to live only in the full nav, or should an extra rail icon (clearly beyond your list) gather them?
  6. Recent tasks. Should the ⌘K palette list your recent tasks before you type, so the rail doesn't lose them?
  7. Remembered per browser. Rail or full is kept with the nav's width in this browser, as the width is today. Or should it follow your account across devices?
